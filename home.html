How a game night goes

You run the game from one phone, from setting it up to the winner. These are the real screens.

  1. 1Before the break

    Set your house rules

    Every pub plays killer a little differently. Pick yours once; the app plays by them all night.

    • Starting lives

      Everyone starts with the same number. Three is usual.

    • 8-ball rule

      Pot the 8 for an extra life, capped at your starting lives or no limit.

    • Shuffle each round

      A new order every round, or keep it fixed.

    • Nobody twice in a row

      When the order changes, nobody shoots two turns back to back.

    • Special cards

      Ball in hand, swap lives, shield and more. Pick which are in play.

    • Confirmation shot

      The last one standing must pot one more to win.

    • Entry fee

      Set what everyone pays in; the app keeps the pot.

    • Venue and title

      Shown on the TV and the follow-along page.

  2. 2Who's playing

    Add the players

    Type each name, or show a QR code so players add themselves. Tap a circle to take their photo, so the room knows who's up at a glance.

  3. 3Every shot

    One tap per shot

    The screen shows who's up and their lives. Tap what happened, confirm, and the next player is up.

  4. 4Round after round

    The app keeps track

    Everyone's lives, a tick for who has shot this round, and who's out. Nobody shoots twice in a row. Mis-tapped? Fix anyone's lives from the roster.

  5. 5Last one standing

    A winner

    See the runner-up and the top three, share the winner card, then play again with the same people.

Late arrivals

Turned up late? They're still in.

Add players after the game has started, even halfway through a round. Open the players list, tap Manage roster and type their name. They join the turn order with full lives, and the TV and the follow-along page update straight away.

No restarting the game, no one waiting for the next one.

Follow along

Watch the game from anywhere in the pub

Outside, at the bar, or knocked out early? Scan the QR code on the pub TV or the organiser's phone with your camera. A live page shows who's up, everyone's lives, who's out, and the winner.
