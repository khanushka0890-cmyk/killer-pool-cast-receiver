Before the break

Your house rules, set once.

Every pub plays killer a little differently. Pick yours and the app plays by them all night.

  • Starting lives

    Everyone starts with the same number. Three is usual.

  • 8-ball rule

    Pot the 8 for an extra life, capped at your starting lives or no limit.

  • Shuffle each round

    A new order every round, or keep it fixed.

  • Nobody twice in a row

    When the order changes, nobody shoots two turns back to back.

  • Special cards

    Ball in hand, swap lives, shield and more. Pick which are in play.

  • Confirmation shot

    The last one standing must pot one more to win.

  • Entry fee

    Set what everyone pays in; the app keeps the pot.

  • Venue and title

    Shown on the TV and the follow-along page.

Who's playing

Everyone in, in a minute.

Type each name, or show a QR code and let players add themselves. Tap a circle to take a photo, so the room knows who's up at a glance.

  • A number and a colour for everyone, photos if you want them
  • Players can join from their own phone with the QR code
  • Your regulars from this venue, one tap each Coming soon

Every shot

One tap per shot. The app does the rest.

The screen shows who's up and their lives. Tap what happened, confirm, and the next player is up.

POTMISS8-BALL POTTED
  • A tick for everyone who has shot this round
  • Nobody shoots twice in a row
  • Mis-tapped? Fix anyone's lives from the roster

Late arrivals

Turned up late? Still in.

Add players after the game has started, even halfway through a round. Open the players list, tap Manage roster and type their name.

  • They join with full lives, at the end of the order
  • No restarting the game, nobody waiting for the next one
  • The TV and the follow-along page update straight away

Follow along

Watch from anywhere in the pub.

Outside, at the bar, or knocked out early? Scan the QR code on the pub TV or the organiser's phone with your camera.

  • Who's up, everyone's lives, who's out, and the winner
  • No app, no sign-up
  • Updates live as the game goes

Last one standing

A winner, and the pot.

See the runner-up and the top three, share the winner card, then play again with the same people.

  • Share the winner card to the group chat
  • Play again keeps everyone, with fresh lives
  • Past games and winners stay on your phone

On the pub TV

The whole room can see who's up.

Cast to a Chromecast on the same Wi-Fi: who's up, who's next, everyone's lives and the pot. Players don't need the app.
